For attraction managers

Connect visitors
with your place.

A proposal for coordinating Cape Coast visits, developed through direct conversations with the people who care for each site.

Good visits begin with clear agreements.

Cape Coast Compass proposes to coordinate visitor communication, city pickup, agreed transport, applicable admission payments and return journeys.

Managers retain control of access, opening hours, capacity, site rules and guiding services. No partnership, endorsement or commercial agreement is currently claimed.

What we need to agree.

  • Access permission and opening hours
  • Admission categories and group capacity
  • Payment, settlement and receipts
  • Guiding and visitor rules
  • Cancellation terms
  • Approved photographs and image permissions

Questions for a first conversation.

Is there a confirmed commission or pricing model?

No. Transport, applicable admission and any disclosed service charge must be agreed. No commission percentage or revenue projection is established.

Does appearing in the directory mean we are partners?

No. The directory is a research collection. Listings do not imply affiliation, endorsement, permission or current tourism availability.

Can the website accept visitors’ bookings?

Not yet. An operator process, booking submission, confirmed availability and final prices must be established before payment is enabled.
