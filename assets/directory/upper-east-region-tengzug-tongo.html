Talensi traditional heritage

Tengzug/Tongo, Upper East Region

The Tengzug–Tongo landscape includes Talensi settlements, traditional compounds and multiple sacred sites. UNESCO’s tentative-list account describes shrines and ancestor-related traditions across this landscape. This community heritage context is broader than the separate Tengzug Shrine listing and does not identify one visitor entrance.
