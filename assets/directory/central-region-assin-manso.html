Assin Manso Ancestral Slave River site

Assin Manso, Central Region

The Assin Manso Ancestral Slave River Park commemorates people forced along the transatlantic slave-trade route. Assin South District Assembly identifies the Last Bath river site, the Memorial Wall of Return and the Ancestral Graveyard within this same park. The memorial and burial grounds are included in this listing rather than counted as separate trip stops.
