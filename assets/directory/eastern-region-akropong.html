Heritage

Akuapem traditional heritage

Akropong, Eastern Region

Akropong is a centre of Akuapem traditions in Ghana’s Eastern Region. Ghana Tourism Authority describes Odwira as a celebration of purification, ancestors and the harvest in Akropong and neighbouring Akuapem towns. This listing covers living community heritage, not a single museum or a confirmed event schedule.
