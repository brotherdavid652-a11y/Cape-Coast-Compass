Heritage

Adinkra cultural heritage

Ntonso, Ashanti Region

Ntonso is associated with adinkra cloth and its symbolic patterns. Ghana Tourism Authority describes a visitor centre and guided activities covering dye preparation, stamping and embroidery. These are living craft traditions; current tour availability and participation arrangements should be confirmed before visiting.
