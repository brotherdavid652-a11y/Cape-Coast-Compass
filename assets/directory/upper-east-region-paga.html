Paga traditional heritage

Paga, Upper East Region

Paga’s heritage includes traditions associated with sacred crocodile ponds and local chiefly institutions. Ghana Tourism Authority distinguishes these community traditions from the nearby Pikworo Slave Camp. This listing covers more than one local place and does not identify a single verified entrance.
