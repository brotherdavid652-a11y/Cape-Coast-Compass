Elmina historic township

Elmina, Central Region

Elmina’s historic township combines coastal community life with the setting of its forts. Ghana Tourism Authority identifies Bakatue as a tradition of Elmina and the Edina traditional area. This town experience is distinct from admission to Elmina Castle; private buildings and sacred activities require their own access arrangements.

Posuban shrines

Elmina, Central Region

Posuban shrines are associated with Fante Asafo companies and are found at several locations in Elmina. This collective listing is not a single enclosed attraction. The map identifies Posuban Shrine 1; it does not represent every shrine. Ask the relevant custodians about access and photography.
