About Cape Coast Compass

Cape Coast Compass is a tourism information website and trip-planning preview for Cape Coast, Ghana. These terms describe use of this website. The operator is presented under the Cape Coast Compass name; no affiliation with an attraction, transport provider or public authority is implied.

Planning is not a booking

Your selected stops, date, visitor count and pickup point form a proposed itinerary. The website does not send booking requests, reserve transport or admission, confirm availability, or take payments. No service contract or reservation is created by using the planner. Do not treat a saved plan as a confirmation.

Attraction information and access

Descriptions and photographs are provided to help you explore possible visits. Opening times, entry charges, permissions, accessibility, capacity and conditions may change. Confirm arrangements with the relevant venue before travelling. Nearby destinations require separate arrangements and are not included in the city planner.

Future service arrangements

Before a paid visit can be offered, the operator and visitor must agree in writing on availability, itinerary, pickup and return arrangements, inclusions, the total price and cancellation terms. Meals, guiding and optional activities are not included unless expressly agreed.
