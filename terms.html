About Tourism Compass

Tourism Compass is a tourism information website and trip-planning platform for destinations across Ghana. These terms describe use of this website. The operator is presented under the Tourism Compass name; no affiliation with an attraction, transport provider or public authority is implied.

Planning is not a booking

Your selected stops, date, visitor count and optional meeting-point notes form a proposed itinerary. The website does not send booking requests, reserve transport or admission, confirm availability, or take payments. No service contract or reservation is created by using the planner. Do not treat a saved plan as a confirmation.

Attraction information and access

Descriptions and photographs are provided to help you explore possible visits. Opening times, entry charges, permissions, accessibility, capacity and conditions may change. Confirm arrangements with the relevant venue before travelling. The planner accepts attractions across the regions represented in the catalogue. Inclusion in a plan does not confirm access or arrange travel.

Future service arrangements

Before a paid visit can be offered, the operator and visitor must agree in writing on availability, itinerary, pickup and return arrangements, inclusions, the total price and cancellation terms. Meals, guiding and optional activities are not included unless expressly agreed.

Respectful and responsible use

Use the site lawfully. Do not interfere with its operation, submit harmful content, or misrepresent another person. At heritage sites, places of worship, markets and coastal communities, follow venue rules, respect residents, and obtain permission before photographing people.

Photographs and website content

Photographs have different sources and reuse conditions. A download link does not grant a commercial licence or override a photographer’s rights. Some supplied images have been refined for colour and brightness; 8K downloads are upscaled and do not represent recovered native 8K detail. Image credits and editing notes are retained where available.

Privacy and third-party websites

Read our Privacy Policy for local trip storage and optional location access. Third-party websites and venues operate under their own policies. Tourism Compass does not control their services.

Changes and questions

These terms may be updated as the service develops. The date below identifies this version. For questions, use the contact channel through which Tourism Compass shared this website with you. Please do not publish private trip details in a public message. Nothing in these terms is intended to exclude rights that applicable law does not allow to be excluded.

Customer accounts

When registering an account, use an email address you control, confirm it when requested, and keep your password private. Creating an account does not create a booking, reserve transport or change the trip planner into a submitted request. Do not use another person’s identity or share account credentials publicly. Contact the operator through the channel used to share this website for account deletion or access concerns.
