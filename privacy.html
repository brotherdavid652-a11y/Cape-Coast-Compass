The current website

Cape Coast Compass currently provides information and a browser-based trip-planning preview. It has no customer accounts, booking database, advertising trackers or payment processing. This policy describes the current implementation, rather than a future booking service.

Trip details stay in your browser

The planner stores chosen attraction identifiers, preferred date, number of visitors and pickup text in browser session storage under cape-compass-trip-v1. This allows your plan to continue across pages in the same browser session. These fields are not submitted to Cape Coast Compass by the current website. They are not a confirmed booking. Browser session restoration may preserve a session after a restart.

Optional location access

The site requests your location only when you press “Use my current location” and your browser grants permission. It fills the pickup field with latitude and longitude and shows estimated accuracy. Coordinates can be retained with your plan in session storage. The site does not continuously track you or turn coordinates into a street address. You may enter a pickup point manually instead.

Hosting and technical information

Cloudflare Pages hosts this website. When you visit, the hosting infrastructure processes technical request data such as IP address, requested URL, browser information and security-related logs to deliver and protect the site. This processing is separate from the local trip planner. See Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy for its practices. Requests may be processed through Cloudflare’s global infrastructure.

Cookies, analytics and payments

The site does not set advertising or analytics cookies and does not install an analytics service. Session storage is used for the planner and is separate from cookies. Hosting or browser security features may have their own behaviour. No card numbers, payment credentials or card payments are collected here.

Your choices and retention

To remove the locally saved itinerary, open My trip and select “Clear my trip”, or clear this site’s storage in your browser. Revoke location permission in browser settings at any time. Clearing the plan does not remove hosting-provider logs. We do not set or claim a retention period for Cloudflare’s infrastructure logs. Avoid entering sensitive information in the pickup field.

Contact and privacy questions

For privacy questions or concerns, contact Cape Coast Compass through the channel used to share this site with you. If a request concerns local trip information, clearing browser storage is the immediate way to remove it; the current planner does not send a copy to the operator. You may also contact Ghana’s Data Protection Commission about data protection concerns. Do not post personal information publicly.

Future changes

A booking, payment, contact form or analytics integration would change how data is processed. This policy will need to be updated before those features collect information, including the operator’s contact details, purposes, recipients and retention arrangements.
