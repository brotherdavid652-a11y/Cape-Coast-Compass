Before you set off.

What can I do now?

Discover places across the represented regions and save selected stops in the browser-based planner. Transport and admission coordination are proposed services, not confirmed nationwide operations.

Can I book or pay today?

No. This is a planning preview. Requests are not sent, visits are not reserved, final prices are unconfirmed, and card payment is unavailable.

Can I plan across different regions?

Yes. Add supported catalogue attractions from different regions to the same plan. This does not create a route, confirm travel times or arrange transport.

What happens to my meeting-point notes?

Meeting-point notes are optional and stay in your current browser session. They do not arrange transport or make a booking. Location is requested only when you press the location button, and you can edit or clear your notes.
