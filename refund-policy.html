Know the terms before you pay.

No payments are accepted today

Tourism Compass currently provides attraction discovery and browser-based trip planning across the regions represented in its Ghana catalogue. It does not accept payments, collect card details, sell admission or confirm bookings. Creating or clearing a plan carries no charge. Because this site currently collects no payment, there is no site payment to cancel or refund.

Your proposed itinerary

You may edit or clear your proposed visit at any time in My trip. This does not cancel a reservation with a venue or another provider because the website has not made one.

Payments to other providers

If you separately book or pay an attraction, hotel, guide or transport provider, its cancellation and refund terms apply to that transaction. Contact the provider that received the payment. A directory listing does not mean Tourism Compass sold, guaranteed or received payment for that service.

Before paid bookings are introduced

The cancellation terms must be supplied and agreed before any future payment. They must specify cancellation deadlines, refund eligibility, any disclosed deductions, treatment of venue charges, changes, no-shows, operator cancellations and the refund method and expected processing time. No cancellation fee, refund percentage or processing deadline is currently established.

Questions or an unexpected charge

Do not submit card details through this website. If you believe a payment has been associated with Tourism Compass, check the payment receipt and contact the named recipient through a verified channel. You may contact Tourism Compass through the channel used to share the site, without posting card or account information publicly.

Policy updates

This policy describes the current preview. It must be replaced or expanded when a payment and booking process is introduced. Any future terms must be disclosed before payment, and nothing here is intended to remove rights that cannot be excluded under applicable law.
