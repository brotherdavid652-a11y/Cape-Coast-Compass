About the museum

Explore Asante royal history in the former residence of Prempeh I, built in 1925 and opened as a museum in 1995. The museum preserves collections connected with Asante kingship and cultural life.

Published visitor information

Opening hours
The museum publishes Monday–Sunday, 9 a.m.–5 p.m., including statutory holidays. Confirm the schedule before travelling.
Access and appointments
The museum FAQ welcomes general visitors and says an appointment is not required for a normal visit. Confirm group visits and any special arrangements directly.
Admission
Current admission fees have not been independently verified here. Check with the museum before your visit.
Photography
The museum permits photography on the grounds but prohibits it inside galleries. Ask staff about designated photography areas and their rules.
Dress
The museum asks visitors to dress casually but decently.
