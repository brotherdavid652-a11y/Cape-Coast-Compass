Visitor information

The Komfo Anokye Sword Site is on the grounds of Komfo Anokye Teaching Hospital in Kumasi. Ghana Tourism Authority connects the sword with Okomfo Anokye and Asante history. The broader historical-heritage listing is included here, rather than offered as a second destination. The sword’s immovability is a traditional belief, not an independently verified claim.
